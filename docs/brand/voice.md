# Voice

The copy bank. The rules are in `README.md` → Voice; this file is what they
look like. The "Do" column is copy the app already ships.

---

## Principles

1. **Talk to one person.** "Your clean streak", never "the user's streak".
2. **Say what happened, then what to do.** One sentence each, at most.
3. **Calm about slips.** A relapse is logged, not judged. A missed day is a
   number, not a failure.
4. **Plain words.** "Two-factor authentication", not "MFA"; "sign in", not
   "authenticate".
5. **Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji.**

## Buttons

A button is a verb, and the verb names exactly what happens.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Save goal | Submit |
| Save changes | OK |
| Log relapse | I failed |
| Remove goal | Delete forever!! |
| Create a habit | Get started now! |

## Errors

What went wrong, then the fix. No apology, no blame, no error codes.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Could not reach the server. Check your connection and try again. | Network error (ERR_503) |
| Could not save that day. | Oops! Something broke 😢 |
| That goal does not exist any more. | 404 Not Found |
| Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. | Invalid TOTP token |

## Empty states

Name the empty thing, then the one next step.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **No habits yet.** Create one to build, or one to break. | You have no data. |
| **No goals yet.** Link a goal to a habit, like a 30-day reading streak or a smoke-free month. | Goals will show up here. |
| **Nothing to count yet.** Statistics appear once you track a habit. | No statistics available |

## Confirmations

The title names the action and its object; the body says what stays and what goes.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Log a relapse on Sep 12?**<br>Your clean streak starts again the next day. You can undo this. | Are you sure? This cannot be undone! |
| **Delete Read**<br>It leaves your lists, and so do the goals linked to it. Your check-ins are kept. | Delete habit? |
| **Remove this goal**<br>The goal is deleted. Its habit and check-ins stay. | Warning: destructive action |

## Success

Past tense, one line, then get out of the way.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Password changed. | Awesome! Your password was successfully updated! 🎉 |
| Two-factor authentication is on. | You're all set, security champion! |

## Hints and placeholders

A placeholder is an example, never the label.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Read 10 pages | Enter habit name here |
| Why this matters to you | Notes (optional, max 500 characters) |
| Pick something small you want to do every day, like reading ten pages. | Habits should be SMART goals. |

## Marketing

The same voice, one step bolder. Lead with the headline, back it with a real
number.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Build the good ones. Break the rest. | Unlock your best self with AI-powered habit optimization! |
| Track habits. Stay consistent. | The #1 habit app you'll ever need |
| One tap a day. Your streak does the rest. | Crush your goals 💪🔥 |
